## 2. Whose information does this notice cover?

This notice covers people who submit the public volunteer interest sheet and people who separately have or use an authenticated volunteer account or portal. The public interest sheet does not create an account, make an application decision, or guarantee a volunteer placement.

The volunteer application is reviewed by HASH. Creating an account or submitting an application does not guarantee approval, placement, or participation.

## 3. What information do we collect?

The public online interest sheet collects:

- **Contact details:** first name, last name, email address, optional pronouns, optional contact number, and your preferred contact method.
- **Acknowledgement record:** confirmation that you saw the short privacy explanation shown with the form.

The printable sheet asks for the same contact details. Paper copies should be secured while in use and securely destroyed within 14 days after collection.

If you later use a separately configured volunteer account or portal, HASH may also process:

- **Identity and account details:** first name, last name, preferred name, email address, account identifier, and account/security information needed to operate sign-in and password recovery.
- **Contact and profile details:** optional phone number, preferred contact method, general location or suburb, and preferred communication language.
- **Application details:** volunteer interests, availability, approximate commitment, skills, experience, motivation, and other information you choose to provide.
- **Volunteer participation details:** expressions of interest in published opportunities and, if you are approved, training progress, announcements, and activity records associated with your account.
- **Acknowledgement records:** whether and when you acknowledged the Code of Conduct, this Privacy Notice, and application communications, including the document URLs presented at the time.
- **Technical and security information:** information processed by the authentication, hosting, and database services to provide, secure, and troubleshoot the portal. The precise logs and retention periods depend on HASH's configured providers and must be confirmed before publication.

The interest sheet does not ask for health information, HIV status, test results, medical records, counselling notes, or information about HASH clients or service users. **Do not include these or other sensitive personal information in free-text fields or on the paper sheet.** If you send such information unintentionally, contact HASH using the privacy contact above so it can assess and handle the request appropriately.

If HASH separately enables a volunteer account, its password is handled by the authentication service for sign-in and is not displayed to HASH staff through the volunteer portal. Never share your password with HASH staff or anyone else.

## 4. Why do we use your information?

For the public interest sheet, HASH uses the information only to:

1. respond to your enquiry about volunteering and contact you using your preferred method; and
2. maintain a short-lived record of your enquiry while HASH follows up.

Information in a separately configured volunteer account or portal may also be used to assess applications and administer accounts, opportunities, training, announcements, participation, and applicable program procedures.

HASH will not use application information for unrelated advertising or sell it. Any materially different use will be explained before it begins, where required by law.

## 5. What is the basis for processing?

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and applicable National Privacy Commission issuances, HASH will identify and document an appropriate lawful basis for each processing purpose. Depending on the purpose and the facts, these may include:

- taking steps at your request before a volunteer arrangement is formed;
- HASH's legitimate interests in receiving applications, administering a volunteer program, protecting people and systems, and maintaining appropriate records, where those interests are not overridden by your rights;
- compliance with a legal obligation, where one applies; and
- your consent where required, including any specific consent legally required before processing sensitive personal information.

The interest sheet asks you to acknowledge the short privacy explanation before submitting. That acknowledgement does not authorize HASH to collect information the sheet does not request or use your information for an unrelated purpose. The separate authenticated portal may request additional acknowledgements before account registration is enabled.

If you choose not to provide information marked optional, HASH may have less information to consider when assessing your application. Information marked required is needed to submit an application or operate the requested account.

## 8. How do we protect your information?

HASH uses access controls, role-based permissions, and other administrative, physical, and technical safeguards appropriate to the information and risks. Access to private volunteer records is restricted through account permissions and database policies. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, but HASH will take reasonable steps to protect information and respond to suspected security incidents in accordance with applicable requirements.

The Volunteer Code of Conduct separately requires volunteers to protect confidential information learned through their work, including after their service ends. It does not replace HASH's own privacy and security responsibilities.

## 9. Your rights and how to make a request

Subject to the Data Privacy Act and its Implementing Rules and Regulations, you may have the right to be informed about processing; access your personal information; object to certain processing; request correction, erasure, or blocking where applicable; request data portability where applicable; and seek damages or lodge a complaint with the National Privacy Commission where the legal requirements are met.

To exercise a right, contact HASH's DPO/privacy contact in Section 1. Please describe your request and provide enough information for HASH to verify your identity and locate the relevant records. HASH may ask for additional information to protect your account and other people. HASH will respond within the period required by applicable law. Some rights may be limited where an exception or legal retention obligation applies; HASH will explain the outcome.

You may also contact the **National Privacy Commission** through its official website at [privacy.gov.ph](https://privacy.gov.ph/) for information about its complaint and assistance processes.
